01OVERVIEW

Forming Professionals at the Intersection of Faith and Technology

The College of Computer Studies is the technology faculty of De La Salle John Bosco College, a Catholic Lasallian institution founded in 1963 in Bislig City, Surigao del Sur. Our programs produce graduates who are not merely technically competent, but who possess the ethical grounding and service orientation that define a Lasallian education.

We believe that technology, at its best, is a means of service to community, nation, and the broader human family. Our curriculum reflects this conviction, combining rigorous computing fundamentals with research, industry engagement, and community extension rooted in the Lasallian charism of faith, service, and community.

Graduates of CCS go on to careers in software development, data science, IT infrastructure, research, and public service, equipped with skills the industry demands and the values society needs.

CORE VALUES

  • FidesFaith as the foundation of ethical computing practice
  • ServitumService to community through technology and skill
  • CommunitasCommunity built through shared knowledge and purpose

02PROGRAMS

Two Pathways in Computing

CCS offers two CHED-recognized four-year degree programs, each tailored to distinct career paths in the computing profession, from deep theoretical work to hands-on systems practice.

Bachelor of Science in Computer Science

A rigorous, theory-grounded program preparing graduates for advanced computing careers in algorithm design, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and software engineering.

AREAS OF FOCUS

  • CS410 Artificial Intelligence
  • CS420 Data Science & Analytics
  • CS430 Cybersecurity Fundamentals
  • CS450 Software Engineering
